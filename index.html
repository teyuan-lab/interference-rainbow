Why a fold

The exit angle rises with impact parameter, peaks at the Descartes ray, then falls. Rays pile up at that maximum, so the wavefront folds into a cusp and geometric optics predicts infinite brightness at θR.

Why fringes

Inside θR every direction is reached by two rays, one on each side of the Descartes ray. Their path difference grows as you move inward, so brightness alternates. Airy's integral gives the shape: intensity ∝ Ai²(−z).

Why big drops hide them

Fringe spacing scales as (λ/a)2/3. For millimetre raindrops the fringes become narrower than the sun's disk and the spread between colours, so they wash out.
